Feed The Valley

Meals, health screenings, and Narcan for Brazos County, run by FFAC.

What It Is

A community relief program, not a one-off drive

Feed The Valley provides healthy meals to people in need across Brazos County, alongside free health screenings, Narcan distribution, and referrals to local care. FFAC buys food in bulk from Restaurant Depot, prepares the meals, and sets up tables in the county's homeless-heavy areas.

Meals

Bulk-cooked, healthy food

Food bought in bulk and prepared into full meals, not just snacks or packaged goods.

Health

Free screenings

Blood pressure, pulse oximetry, hunger vital signs, and BMI, checked at the same table.

Health

Narcan distribution

Free naloxone available on-site, no questions asked.

Referrals

Connections to care

Anyone who needs follow-up care gets referred to a local clinic that matches what they can pay.
