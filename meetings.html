Where we've been

SEP9

First General Meeting

When: Wednesday, 9/9/2026, 5:30–6:30 PM  •  Where: Koldus Building, Room 146

Our kickoff meeting for the semester. Flip through the slides below.

What we covered

  • Who we are: a social-service org and nonprofit that brings food lovers together and fights food insecurity in the Brazos Valley.
  • Meet the officers: all ten officers introduced themselves.
  • Social and service: potlucks, cookouts, and restaurant outings, plus volunteering with the Brazos Valley Food Bank, other food banks and kitchens, and the Aggieland Humane Society.
  • Upcoming events: officers pitched our bake sales, volunteer shifts, and socials.
  • Membership: 3 meetings, 1 fundraiser, 1 service event, and 2 Feed The Valley events per semester, plus $20 semester dues.
  • Feed The Valley: our new program that cooks healthy meals from bulk ingredients and hands them out, with free health screenings and Narcan, in homeless-heavy areas of the Brazos Valley.
