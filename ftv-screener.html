Feed The Valley: Associate Screener

Saturday, December 5, 2026, 1–3 PM, 903 N Washington Ave, Bryan, TX 77803.

5Associate Screeners Needed

Associate Screeners take basic vitals (blood pressure, temperature, pulse ox, and similar) at our free health screening table. Screening runs 1–3 PM with food handout. Cooking is a separate sign-up (9–11:30 AM).

No experience or certification? You will be sent an email 1 week before the event for a training course on taking vitals. The training takes 1 hour.
