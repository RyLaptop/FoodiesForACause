About Feed The Valley
Texas A&M · Since 2025

Food is service. Food is community.

Foodies For A Cause is a social service organization at Texas A&M built around food: volunteering with local food banks, raising money for hunger-relief organizations, and learning what food means across culture, history, and science.

Our Mission

Service First, Food Always

FFAC pairs hands-on service with a genuine love of food. We volunteer, we fundraise, and we learn. Then we get together to enjoy the thing that brought us here in the first place.

Service

Volunteering

Members work directly with food banks and hunger-relief organizations across Bryan-College Station.

Service

Fundraisers

Bake sales and other fundraisers put real dollars behind organizations like the BV Food Bank.

Service

Feed The Valley

Our own program: bulk-cooked meals, free health screenings, and Narcan distribution for Brazos County's homeless-heavy areas. First event September 12.
Learning

Themed Meetings

Every meeting looks at food from a new angle: other cultures, food history, the science of cooking, and food marketing.

Learning

Cooking & Baking Classes

Hands-on sessions where members build real kitchen skills and try new recipes together.

Social

Potlucks & Cookouts

Regular potlucks and cookouts bring the group together to share food and build community.

Local Food Spots
